## Loss analysis (scripts/analyze_losses.py; dev BTC/ETH/SOL 2021-2023, 2% risk, one $10k account per coin)
- W1 (4h EMA pullback), 718 trades, net +$24,019, fees $2,567, funding -$2,641:
  longs +$22,084 (mean 0.44R), shorts +$1,935 (mean 0.02R). Shorts roughly break even after
  costs, so they add fees and drawdown. Trades against the 200-day trend did better
  (0.49R) than with it (0.09R), so a 200-day trend filter would cut the better trades and
  is not used. Low-volatility trades paid twice the fees (tight stops, big size) for a
  similar result. Stop-loss exits: 66 trades, -$9,307; strategy exits: 651 trades, +$33,383.

- T2 (Williams volatility breakout; finished after the dev runs, read before any final result),
  1,760 trades, net +$7,306: fees $12,213 took 60% of the $20,342 gross. Shorts ~0R; SOL lost;
  high-volatility days lost (-0.03R). Fees, not direction, are T2's main loss.

## Step targets and selection (fixed now)
A step is reached on a test when the geometric average month (`average_month_pct`) is at
least 10%, 20%, 30% or 50%. Development steps are not evidence.

1. Among candidate x risk runs with no losing development year and max drawdown <= 35%, take
   the highest average month.
2. Otherwise the highest average month with max drawdown <= 35%.
3. Otherwise the best return / max drawdown.

The rule level and the step reached are reported; the choice is frozen by commit before the
validation and final runs.

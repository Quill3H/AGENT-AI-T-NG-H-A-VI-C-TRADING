## 2. Logic Chain

### 2.1 The Mathematics of Fee Drag & Stop-Loss Floor
1. **Mathematical Derivation**:
   Let $S$ be the stop loss distance as a percentage of entry price: $S = \frac{|P_{entry} - P_{stop}|}{P_{entry}}$.  
   Let $F = 0.0016$ ($0.16\%$) be the round-trip fee and slippage friction.  
   Let $k$ be the target R-multiple ($k = 1.5$ for TP1, $k = 3.0$ for TP2).  
   Let $W$ be the win rate.
   
   - Gross Win at $k R$: $G_W = k \cdot S$.
   - Net Win at $k R$: $N_W = k \cdot S - F$.
   - Gross Loss at $-1 R$: $G_L = -S$.
   - Net Loss at $-1 R$: $N_L = -(S + F)$.

   For positive net mathematical expectancy ($E[PnL] > 0$):
   $$E[PnL] = W \cdot (k \cdot S - F) - (1 - W) \cdot (S + F) > 0$$
   $$W \cdot k \cdot S - W \cdot F - S - F + W \cdot S + W \cdot F > 0$$
   $$S \cdot [W \cdot (k + 1) - 1] - F > 0$$
   $$S > \frac{F}{W \cdot (k + 1) - 1}$$

2. **Empirical Numerical Proof**:
   - Case A: $W = 0.50$ (50% win rate), $k = 1.5$ (TP1 at 1.5R):
     $$S > \frac{0.0016}{0.50 \cdot (1.5 + 1) - 1} = \frac{0.0016}{0.25} = 0.0064 = \mathbf{0.64\%}$$
   - When $S = 0.20\%$ (the observed pullback stop distance):
     - Net Win: $1.5 \times 0.20\% - 0.16\% = +0.14\%$.
     - Net Loss: $-0.20\% - 0.16\% = -0.36\%$.
     - Net Win/Loss Ratio: $\frac{0.14\%}{0.36\%} = \mathbf{0.389}$!
     - Expectancy: $0.50 \times (+0.14\%) + 0.50 \times (-0.36\%) = -0.11\%$ per trade. Over 1,000 trades, this guarantees $-110\%$ loss!
   - Case B: Enforce an ATR-based Minimum Stop Distance $S \ge S_{min} = 1.0\% - 1.2\%$:
     - When $S = 1.20\%$:
       - Net Win at 1.5R: $1.5 \times 1.20\% - 0.16\% = \mathbf{+1.64\%}$.
       - Net Loss at -1.0R: $-1.20\% - 0.16\% = \mathbf{-1.36\%}$.
       - Net Payoff Ratio: $\frac{1.64\%}{1.36\%} = \mathbf{1.206}$!
       - Fee Drag as a fraction of gross profit drops from $53.3\%$ down to $\frac{0.16\%}{1.80\%} = \mathbf{8.89\%}$!
       - Net Win at 3.0R: $3.0 \times 1.20\% - 0.16\% = \mathbf{+3.44\%}$ (Payoff Ratio: $2.53$).

3. **Inference**:
   To absorb the 0.16% fee drag, the strategy must enforce a **Dynamic ATR Stop Floor**:
   $$StopDistance = \max\left(\Delta_{structure}, 1.2 \times ATR_{14}, 0.008 \times P_{entry}\right)$$
   guaranteeing $S \ge 0.80\%$ to $1.20\%$ across all 10 pairs.

### 2.2 Sizing Guardrail & Margin Invariant Elimination

   Account Equity $E = \$50,000$. Leverage $L = 5.0x$ to $10.0x$. Risk percent $r \in [0.010, 0.015]$.
   $$PositionSize = \frac{E \cdot r}{S}, \quad RequiredMargin = \frac{PositionSize}{L} = \frac{E \cdot r}{S \cdot L}$$

   $$\frac{E \cdot r}{S \cdot L} \le E \iff S \ge \frac{r}{L}$$

## 3. Caveats

1. **Synthetic vs Real Public Funding Rates**:
   - The historical Parquet dataset in `data/raw/binance/` contains historical 15m OHLCV. Where historical 8h funding rate snapshots are missing, the pipeline defaults to $+0.01\%$ baseline per 8h (`candle_15m['funding_rate'] = 0.0001`). In real market conditions, funding rates spike to $+0.05\% - +0.10\%$ during extreme bull runs, activating the `funding_filter` to veto crowded longs.
2. **Order Execution Assumption**:
   - PaperBroker executes market orders at the open price of the subsequent candle with $0.03\%$ linear adverse slippage. In extreme liquidity shocks (e.g. flash crashes), slippage on altcoins may exceed $0.03\%$. However, because minimum stop distance is set to $\ge 0.80\% - 1.20\%$, the strategy maintains a healthy safety buffer.
3. **Cross-Asset Correlation**:
   - During major Bitcoin macro impulses, all 9 altcoins can exhibit simultaneous directionality. The portfolio-level risk is protected by the maximum 1 concurrent position per symbol rule, isolated margin, and the broker's circuit breaker (`daily_loss_limit_pct: 0.05`).

1. **Zero altcoin execution** was caused by missing `leverage_brackets` in configuration for the 9 altcoin pairs, triggering 30,130 automatic sizing rejections.

3. **Severe fee bleed** ($-\$11,958$) was caused by stop losses being too narrow relative to the $0.16\%$ round-trip fee friction.

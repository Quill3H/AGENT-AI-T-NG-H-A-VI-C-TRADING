Rounds 1-13 all bet on direction (trend, breakouts, candles, relative strength). The ledger
has no market-neutral relative-value test. Round 14 copies the best-known published one: the
distance method of Gatev, Goetzmann and Rouwenhorst, "Pairs Trading: Performance of a
Relative-Value Arbitrage Rule" (Review of Financial Studies 2006). In US stocks 1962-2002 it
reported annualised excess returns of up to 11% for self-financing pair portfolios (summary:
https://alphaarchitect.com/pairs-trading-kicks-butt/). On crypto, a Charles University
thesis (Binance 2018, daily and hourly; https://dspace.cuni.cz/handle/20.500.11956/109634)
found the distance method near 3% a month under its baseline costs, the cointegration method
slightly loss-making, and most parameter sets unprofitable after costs.

## Rules copied (src/strategies/pairs.py)
- Formation: normalise each coin's 4h closes to 1 at the window start; rank the 45 pairs of
  the top-10 by the sum of squared differences of normalised prices; keep the closest
  `top_pairs`. Sigma = the standard deviation of the normalised spread over the formation.
- Trading: renormalise at the last formation close; when the spread exceeds 2 sigma, short
  the high leg and buy the low leg; close at the next crossing or at the end of the window; a
  pair may reopen in the same window. Windows start on the 1st of the month every
  `trading_months` from 2020-09-01, and a window is used only when its formation is fully
  covered by data.
- Futures adaptation (ours, fixed now): each coin is one paper sleeve with $1,000; a coin's
  side is the sign of the sum of its legs over the open pairs (opposite legs net to flat).
  Entry only on the bar where the side changes; exit when it changes away. Every entry has a
  stop of 3 daily ATR(14), as the engine requires a stop on every trade. Sizing is by risk,
  so the two legs are matched in risk, not in dollars. A stopped leg is not re-entered until
  its side changes again.

## Data, costs and risk
- Development: top-10 2021-01-01..2023-12-31 at 2% and 5% risk per trade. P1 needs a full
  12-month formation; its first trading window that opens a pair is in 2022, so its 2021
  return is 0 and it cannot meet the "no losing year" test (strictly positive years).
- Costs 0.05% taker + 0.03% slippage per side, funding at 00/08/16 UTC. Leverage 2x, order cap
  2x equity, liquidation buffer, 5% daily-loss breaker (realized cash flows, per sleeve), stop
  on every trade. No limit is relaxed. The streak rule halves risk after 3 losses.
- After the freeze, checks are on **seen** data only and labelled as context: top-10
  2024-01..2026-09 (and with costs x2), 2026-10-01..17, and C1 on the same top-10 at the same
  risk. New evidence comes only from forward paper from 2026-10-18.

**Disclosure.** Before this plan was committed, a wiring check computed the side series on
the development data (no orders, no P&L): first active bar 2022-09-17 for P1 and 2021-01-02
for P2/P3; 27, 205 and 304 side changes in 2021-2023.

## Selection (fixed now; the round-8 rule)
1. Among P1-P3 x risk with no losing development year and max drawdown <= 35%, take the
   highest geometric average month.
2. Otherwise the highest average month with max drawdown <= 35%.
3. Otherwise the best return / max drawdown.
The result also reports whether the pick beats C1 on the same top-10 at the same risk. The
choice is frozen by commit before any check.

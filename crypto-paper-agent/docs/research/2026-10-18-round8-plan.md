## Step targets and selection (fixed now)
A step is reached on a test when the geometric average month (`average_month_pct`) is at
least 10%, 20%, 30% or 50%. Development steps are not evidence.

1. Among candidate x risk runs with no losing development year and max drawdown <= 35%, take
   the highest average month.
2. Otherwise the highest average month with max drawdown <= 35%.
3. Otherwise the best return / max drawdown.

The rule level and the step reached are reported; the choice is frozen by commit before the
validation and final runs.

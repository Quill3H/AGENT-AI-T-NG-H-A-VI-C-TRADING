## Rules (src/strategies/ichimoku_suite.py)
Goichi Hosoda's standard settings: Tenkan 9, Kijun 26, Senkou B 52, displacement 26. The
cloud under bar t comes from spans computed at t-26; the Chikou check is close[t] vs
close[t-26]. Nothing reads a future bar. Exit: a close back across the Kijun. Stop: 0.5
ATR(14) beyond the Kijun (I1) or beyond the far side of the cloud (I2).
- I1 Tenkan/Kijun cross: Tenkan crosses above Kijun on this bar, the close is above the
  cloud, and the Chikou is above the close 26 bars ago -> long. Mirror for shorts.
- I2 cloud (Kumo) breakout: the previous close was at or under the cloud top, this close is
  above it, the projected cloud is bullish (span A > span B), Tenkan > Kijun and the Chikou
  confirms -> long. Mirror for shorts.

Candidates: I1 and I2 on 4h, 1h and 15m signal bars (15m execution), plus I1-4h and I2-4h long
only (round 8: W1's profit came from longs). Eight candidates at 2% and 5% risk.

## Selection (fixed now; the round-8 rule)
1. Among candidate x risk runs with no losing development year and max drawdown <= 35%, take
   the highest geometric average month.
2. Otherwise the highest average month with max drawdown <= 35%.
3. Otherwise the best return / max drawdown.
Steps of 10/20/30/50% a month are reported. The choice is frozen by commit before the final.
